Security

Built so your data stays yours.

Security isn’t a feature we added later — it’s in the database design.

🔒 Tenant isolation in the database

Every record belongs to a business, and Postgres Row Level Security checks membership on every read and write. Another business can’t see your data — even if someone tampered with the app.

👥 Roles & permissions

Owner, admin, manager, employee and viewer. Permissions are enforced on the server, not just hidden buttons.

🧾 Audit trail

Created, edited and deleted transactions, stock adjustments, price changes and team changes are logged. Financial records are soft-deleted with a reason, never silently erased.

🤖 Privacy-first AI

AI providers are called only from our servers; keys never live on your phone. The assistant receives only the sentence you typed plus today’s date and currency — numbers are calculated by the database. AI output is validated and can’t write data directly.

💳 No card data

Payments are handled by Google Play or our payment partner. BusinessPilot never sees or stores card numbers.

📤 Your data, portable

Export everything as CSV, PDF or a full JSON backup at any time.
