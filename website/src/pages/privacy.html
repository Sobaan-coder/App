To provide the service: storing your records, calculating reports, sending notifications you enable, and answering your questions. We don’t sell your data.

AI processing

When you use the assistant, the sentence you typed (plus your business currency and date) is sent from our servers to an AI provider to understand it. Answers to questions are calculated from your records by our database; only those computed figures may be sent to the AI to phrase an answer. AI providers are contractually prohibited from training on your data (configure per provider).
