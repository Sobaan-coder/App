FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does the AI make up numbers?

No. Every figure comes from your recorded data and is calculated by the database. If something can’t be calculated — like profit without cost prices — BusinessPilot says so instead of guessing.

What if it misunderstands me?

You always see a preview before anything is saved, and the assistant asks when something is missing or ambiguous. Every entry can be undone or deleted, and changes are kept in an audit log.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Manual entries are saved on your device and synced automatically when you’re back online, without duplicates.

Which currencies are supported?

PKR by default, plus USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, INR and many more. Each business picks its own currency and timezone.
