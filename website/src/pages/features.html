Features

One app for sales, stock, expenses and balances.

Talk to it like you’d talk to your accountant — or use quick forms. Both write to the same secure records.

Start free
💬

AI transaction entry

“Sold 5 zinger burgers for Rs 2500 cash”, “Bought 10kg chicken for 5200”, “Paid worker Ahmed 25000”, “Ali owes me 3000”. You see a structured preview and confirm. Low-risk entries can be recorded instantly with Undo — large amounts and payments always ask first.

❓

Ask about your business

“How much did I sell today?”, “Who owes me money?”, “What did I spend on chicken this month?”, “Which product sells the most?” Answers are calculated from your data, not invented.

📊

Dashboard

Sales, expenses, estimated profit, receivables, payables, low stock, trends and top products. Filter by today, yesterday, 7 or 30 days, this or last month, or any dates.

💡

Transparent profit

Revenue − cost of goods sold − operating expenses, with the method shown. If cost data is missing we tell you rather than guess.

📦

Inventory

Opening stock, purchases, sales, waste, damage, returns and adjustments — every change is recorded with a reason. Low-stock alerts and barcode scanning.

👥

Customers & suppliers

Credit sales, payments, outstanding balances, history and one-tap WhatsApp reminders.

🧾

Invoices

Professional PDF invoices with your logo, tax and discounts — or a WhatsApp-ready text receipt via the share sheet.

📈

Reports & exports

Sales, expenses, profit, cash flow, product performance, inventory, customers and suppliers. Export to CSV or PDF.

📶

Offline mode

Keep recording without internet. Entries sync automatically and safely when you reconnect.

🔐

Team roles

Owner, admin, manager, employee and viewer. Staff record sales; only managers change prices or delete records.

📥

Import & export

Import products, customers and opening balances from CSV with validation before anything is saved. Export everything anytime.

🌙

Made for real life

Dark mode, large touch targets, screen-reader labels, multi-currency, timezone-aware reports, and Urdu/Arabic-ready layouts.
