FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is BusinessPilot?

An AI-powered business manager for small businesses. You type what happened — a sale, a purchase, an expense, who owes you — and BusinessPilot records it, updates stock and keeps your dashboard current.

Does the AI ever invent numbers?

No. Figures are calculated by the database from your records. If something can’t be calculated, BusinessPilot tells you what’s missing.

Will it record something without asking?

Only if you switch on instant recording for simple, low-risk entries — and you can always undo. Payments, large amounts, new contacts and anything uncertain always need your confirmation.

Can I still use normal forms?

Yes. Every action has a manual form too. Both methods write to the same records.

Does it work offline?

Manual entries are saved on your device and sync automatically, without duplicates, when you reconnect. The AI assistant needs internet.

Which devices are supported?

Android phones and tablets, and any modern web browser on desktop or mobile.

Which currencies are supported?

PKR, USD, GBP, EUR, AED, SAR, INR and many more. Each business sets its own currency and timezone.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Invite team members with roles that control what they can see and do.

How is profit calculated?

Revenue minus the cost of goods sold minus operating expenses. If cost prices are missing for some sales, we show that profit is unavailable rather than guessing.

Can I export my data?

Yes — CSV and PDF reports, and a full JSON export of your business at any time.

How much does it cost?

There’s a free plan. Paid plans add unlimited records, PDF invoices, more team members and advanced reports. See the pricing page.
